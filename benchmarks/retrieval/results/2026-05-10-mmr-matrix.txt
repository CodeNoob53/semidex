## Interpretation

- `ollama-mmr0.3` is the best tradeoff in this run: same Recall@1 as RRF,
  slightly higher MRR, and lower duplicate source rate.
- `onnx-rrf` remains the strongest relevance baseline: best Recall@1, MRR, and
  nDCG@5.
- Higher MMR diversity values (`0.5`, `0.7`) improve diversity but start
  pushing exact provider queries (`q2`, `q15`) down or out of top-5.
- For semidex production search, this supports keeping hybrid RRF as default.
  MMR is useful as an evaluation target and possible opt-in mode only after
  defining an acceptable relevance regression budget.

Note: ONNX MMR average latency includes cold-start cost in separate benchmark
subprocesses. Warm per-query latency is closer to the individual non-first query
times.
